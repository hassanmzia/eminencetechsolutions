## 3. LLM & AI Discoverability

### What Was Implemented (Technical)

1. **llms.txt** - Machine-readable file at `/llms.txt` that AI crawlers can read to understand your company. This is an emerging standard that helps AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity learn about and recommend your company.

2. **JSON-LD Structured Data** - Rich schema markup in your HTML that helps both search engines and AI systems understand:
   - Organization details (name, founder, address, contacts)
   - All 10 services offered
   - FAQ content (appears as rich snippets in Google)
   - Professional service classification

3. **robots.txt** - Explicitly allows AI crawlers (GPTBot, Claude-Web, PerplexityBot, etc.) to index your site.

### Additional Steps for LLM Visibility

1. **Crunchbase Profile** - Create/update at https://www.crunchbase.com
   - AI systems often cite Crunchbase for company information

2. **Wikipedia** - If eligible, create a Wikipedia article (requires notability)
   - AI models heavily reference Wikipedia content

3. **Wikidata** - Create an entry at https://www.wikidata.org
   - Structured data source used by many AI systems

4. **High-Authority Backlinks** - Get mentioned on:
   - Industry publications (VentureBeat, TechCrunch, etc.)
   - Partner company websites
   - Conference speaker pages
   - Industry association directories

5. **Publish Research** - AI systems prioritize:
   - Medium articles, Substack posts
   - Research papers (ArXiv if applicable)
   - Detailed technical blog posts with unique insights
